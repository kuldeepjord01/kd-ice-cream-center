About KD Ice Cream

Crafting happiness, one scoop at a time since 2015

Our Story

KD Ice Cream started as a small family-owned shop in the heart of Hyderabad. What began as a passion for creating the perfect scoop has grown into a beloved brand known for quality, creativity, and pure joy.

We believe that every scoop tells a story — from the carefully sourced ingredients to the smiles on our customers' faces. Our commitment to freshness and flavour is what sets us apart.
